# Brew Finder (Android APK Project)

## How to Build the `.apk` for Your Phone
1. Open **Android Studio** and select **Open** -> choose this `BelroseBeerFinder` folder.
2. Wait for Gradle to sync (requires internet once to download `androidx.appcompat` and `material`).
3. In the top menu, click **Build > Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) > Build APK(s)**.
4. Once complete, click **locate** in the notification to grab `app-debug.apk` and transfer/install it on your Android phone.

## Features Included
- **Location-first Belrose bottle shop list**: Bayfield's Belrose (primary test case), BWS Glenrose, Dan Murphy's Frenchs Forest, Liquorland Frenchs Forest, and Liquorland Warehouse Forestville.
- **6-Pack & 24-Pack Price Comparison**: Highlights the cheapest store, exact dollar savings, $/bottle, and $/litre.
- **Quick Tap Filters**: Beer style (Pale Ale, Lager, IPA, Stout, XPA), strength (3.5% Mid-Strength vs Full Strength), and Brewery Ownership (Independent Brewers vs Major Commercial).
- **Deal Expiration Countdowns**: Shows when each special ends so you can stock up before prices go back up.
- **Beer Brand Logos & Bayfield's Test-Case Flyer Sync**: Custom brand badges and direct links to live store flyers plus an in-app deal logger saved on your phone.
